New Game Mochi NFT Leaderboard Rules

Rules

GuessPicture

  1. Draw an image on the canvas.
  2. Click “Create game” to save and generate a link.
  3. Share the link with friends to guess your drawing.

DrawMatch

  1. Set a theme for the drawing.
  2. Click “Create game” to save and generate a link.
  3. Share the link with friends to draw a picture.

AiQuiz

  1. Set a quiz questions.
  2. Click “Create game” to save and generate a link.
  3. Share the link with friends to test their knowledge.

PunchLine

  1. Set a first line of a joke.
  2. Click “Create game” to save and generate a link.
  3. Share the link with friends to test their sense of humor.

CatBeauty

  1. Take a photo of your cat.
  2. Upload photo and click “Join contest” to get a beauty score.
  3. Share the result to show how cute your pet is.

AI contest criteria:

  • Full visibility of the cat.
  • Direction of gaze.
  • Sharpness and photo quality.
  • Face symmetry.
  • Expressiveness of the eyes.
  • “Cuteness of the smile”.
  • Ears — “radar of cuteness”.
  • Cleanliness and grooming of fur.
  • Fluffiness / texture.
  • Interesting coat pattern.
  • Elegance of the pose.
  • “Paw cuteness”.
  • Tail — “charisma indicator”.
  • Readable emotion.
  • “Meme potential”.
  • Charisma / charm.
  • Cleanliness of the background.
  • Color harmony.
  • Interesting objects around.

DogBeauty

  1. Take a photo of your dog.
  2. Upload photo and click “Join contest” to get a beauty score.
  3. Share the result to show how cute your pet is.

AI contest criteria:

  • Full visibility of the dog.
  • Direction of gaze.
  • Sharpness and photo quality.
  • Face symmetry and proportions.
  • Expressiveness of the eyes.
  • Friendliness / “smile”.
  • Ears — harmony and character (assess relative to the natural ear type: prick, semi‑prick, floppy, cropped, etc., and to typical expression for this breed/type).
  • Cleanliness and grooming of coat.
  • Coat texture / volume (consider both smooth‑coated and long‑haired dogs relative to their natural type).
  • Interesting coat color and pattern.
  • Elegance and clarity of the pose.
  • Paw cuteness / expressiveness.
  • Tail — “charisma indicator” (assess according to natural tail type and cropping, if present).
  • Readable emotion.
  • “Meme potential”.
  • Charisma / charm.
  • Cleanliness of the background.
  • Color harmony.
  • Interesting objects around.

CookItUp

  1. Click “Create game” to generate a task with random set of ingredients.
  2. Share the link with friends to test their culinary skills.
  3. Create your own recipe with ingredients you have.
