New Game Leaderboard Rules

Rules

GuessPicture

  1. Draw an image on the canvas.
  2. Click “Create game” to save and generate a link.
  3. Share the link with friends to guess your drawing.

DrawMatch

  1. Set a theme for the drawing.
  2. Click “Create game” to save and generate a link.
  3. Share the link with friends to draw a picture.

AiQuiz

  1. Set a quiz questions.
  2. Click “Create game” to save and generate a link.
  3. Share the link with friends to test their knowledge.

PunchLine

  1. Set a first line of a joke.
  2. Click “Create game” to save and generate a link.
  3. Share the link with friends to test their sense of humor.

CatBeauty

  1. Take a photo of your cat.
  2. Upload photo and click “Join contest” to get a beauty score.
  3. Share the result to show how cute your pet is.

DogBeauty

  1. Take a photo of your dog.
  2. Upload photo and click “Join contest” to get a beauty score.
  3. Share the result to show how cute your pet is.

CookItUp

  1. Click “Create game” to generate a task with random set of ingredients.
  2. Share the link with friends to test their culinary skills.
  3. Create your own recipe with ingredients you have.
